About

I am currently completing a PhD at the School of Public Health and Preventive Medicine at Monash University in Melbourne, Australia.

My research focuses on advancing methods to optimise the impact and reduce waste of musculoskeletal health research. In particular, I am working on refining and validating the Australia and New Zealand Musculoskeletal Clinical Trials Network Research Question Importance Tool (ANZMUSC-RQIT), which helps prioritise research that matters most to patients, clinicians, and other stakeholders. This work is supported by the Australian government, Monash University, and ANZMUSC.

Alongside my PhD, I contribute to Cochrane living systematic reviews that inform the Australian Living Guidelines for the Pharmacological Management of Inflammatory Arthritis.

Outside of research, I enjoy the ocean, good coffee, running, discussing faith and beliefs, and spending time with family and friends.
